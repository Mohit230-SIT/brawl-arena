About BRAWL

We're not building a debate app. We're building a status economy where intellectual courage outranks followers.

The Vision

The first status economy where a sharp mind beats a big following.

On every other platform, clout flows to whoever has the most followers, the best camera, the prettiest feed. Brawl rewards a single currency that can't be faked: the quality of a mind under pressure, in sixty seconds, with no preparation.

The quiet kid in the last row — the one with no Instagram following, no podcast, no personal brand — can dethrone the campus celebrity live, at 9 PM, in front of everyone who matters to them. Not because they're popular. Because they're sharper.

Brawl is the first status economy where intellectual courage outranks followers.

The campus product is the marketing. The enterprise product — a B2B communication gym where companies pay to make their employees think and speak clearly under pressure — is the business.

Every feature decision is testable against one question: does this make sharp thinking win? If yes, keep it. If no, it dies.

Architecture

Two streams. One socket. One leaderboard.

The entire product is four blocks. A team can prototype it this weekend. Sub-second latency is non-negotiable.

Media Layer
WebRTC × 2 → SFU → Viewers
Two debaters stream via WebRTC peer connections. A Selective Forwarding Unit fans the streams out to hundreds of viewers without mesh networking.
WebRTC LiveKit (SFU) STUN/TURN
Voting Layer
WebSocket → Redis Counter
Real-time vote taps stream over WebSocket. A Redis counter aggregates live. One vote per user, enforced server-side.
Socket.IO Redis Server-side validation
Prompt Engine
LLM + Curated Bank + Safety Filter
Prompts generated by an LLM from a curated seed bank, filtered for safety. The filter is a feature, not bureaucracy.
LLM API Curated seed bank Safety classifier
State Layer
Redis Sorted Set → Postgres → S3
Live campus ranking in a Redis sorted set. Persistent state and match history in Postgres. Replay clips to object storage.
Redis ZSET PostgreSQL S3 / object storage
The 60-Second Demo

The moment that wins the room.

During the pitch, the CEO draws DEFEND live. Sixty seconds, standing, no notes.

Defender Prompt: "Remote work is killing ambition." ~58 seconds spoken

Sixty seconds. Here's the truth nobody in this room wants to hear: your ambition didn't die on Zoom. It died the day you realized nobody was watching.

Office ambition was never ambition. It was performance — the early arrival, the loud idea in the standup, the face you made visible. Remote work didn't kill drive. It killed the audience for your drive, and that terrified you.

If your ambition only existed because a manager could see it, that wasn't ambition. That was surveillance responding to incentive.

Real ambition doesn't need a witness. The people still rising right now, from their kitchens, at 9 PM — they're the ones whose fire was never about the room.

Remote work didn't kill ambition. It exposed how many of us never had any.

HOLD EYE CONTACT FOR TWO SECONDS. THEN: "THAT'S A BRAWL. THAT'S THE PRODUCT."
