How to Play

Everything you need to enter the arena, fight your corner, and climb the clout board.

Getting Started

Five steps to your first Brawl.

01

Create your fighter profile

Sign up with your campus email. Pick a handle. You start with 1,000 clout — the same as everyone else. No head starts.

02

Wait for 9 PM

The Brawl drops every night at 21:00 IST. If you're in the queue, you might be selected. The app notifies you three seconds before going live.

03

Get your prompt and side

The AI assigns a controversial prompt and randomly assigns you to attack or defend it. You don't choose your side. That's the point.

04

Fight for 60 seconds

Go live. Make your case. No prep, no notes, no tabs. The campus watches and votes in real-time. Every second counts.

05

Win or lose clout

The fighter with the most votes wins. Winner steals up to 50 clout from the loser. The replay clip is auto-generated and shareable.

06

Climb the leaderboard

Keep winning. Climb the campus ranks. Qualify for inter-campus tournaments. Your clout is the only currency that matters.

Strategy

Tips from the top of the board.

  • Hook in the first five seconds. The first sentence sets the tone. Voters form an impression in under five seconds. Open with a reframe, not an apology.
  • Don't repeat the prompt. The audience already heard it. Use those seconds to build your case, not echo the question.
  • Pick one argument and hammer it. Sixty seconds can hold one idea well, or three ideas badly. Depth beats breadth every time.
  • End on a quotable line. The last sentence is what gets clipped and shared. Make it the one people screenshot.
  • Acknowledge the strong point, then demolish it. Strawmanning loses votes. Engaging the best version of the opponent's argument and beating it anyway wins respect.
  • Watch your opponent's Brawls. Every replay is public. Study how the top fighters structure their 60 seconds. Patterns repeat.
  • Vote strategically as an audience member. You earn clout for correct votes. Judge the argument, not the person. Bias costs you clout.
  • Don't forfeit. Ever. The −75 penalty takes three wins to recover. Even a bad Brawl teaches you something. Show up.
FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Can I choose which side I'm on?
No. The AI randomly assigns you to attack or defend the prompt. This is the core mechanic — you must be able to argue a position you didn't choose. That's what makes Brawl a communication trainer, not just an opinion echo chamber.
What if I freeze on camera?
It happens. You still get credit for showing up. The replay stays private unless you choose to share it. And the next night's Brawl is a fresh start — no one remembers yesterday's stumble.
Can my friends vote for me?
They can, but it costs them clout if you lose. The wagering system discourages friend-bias: if your friend gives a weak argument, voting for them out of loyalty loses you clout. The system pushes people to vote honestly.
What happens if I miss my assigned Brawl?
If you're in the queue and get selected but don't show within 3 seconds, it counts as a forfeit (−75 clout). If you know you can't make it, remove yourself from the queue before 9 PM.
Is there an age limit or campus restriction?
Brawl launches campus-first. You need a valid campus email to register for your campus leaderboard. Phase 2 opens inter-campus Brawls. Phase 3 opens the enterprise platform for professionals.
Can I see my old Brawls?
Yes. Every Brawl is recorded and available in your fighter profile. You can mark replays as private or public. Public replays can be shared — they're your portfolio of live argument.
